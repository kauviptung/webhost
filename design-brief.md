# HTL 16666 Media - Design Brief

Design read: An English-language corporate communications website for decision-makers who need a credible, accountable Vietnamese media partner with creative range.

Concept spine: "The Signal Registry" - an institutional dossier meets a broadcast signal system. Every section reveals another verified layer of one coherent company entity rather than an agency portfolio.

Delivery tier: cinema
Animation mode: animated-website
Journey shape: single-shot - one continuous camera film keeps the company represented as one accountable entity.

Journey:
1. ENTITY - a monumental signal core in a controlled architectural studio. Headline: "Built to be accountable." Proof: tax code, active status, Hanoi.
2. INSIGHT - analytical layers and calibrated light paths reveal research and communications consulting.
3. CREATIVE - the same core unfolds into graphic planes and tactile design surfaces for advertising and creative design.
4. ACTIVATION - the installation expands into precise event and trade-promotion architecture.
5. CONTACT - signal lines converge on one core to resolve into one accountable media partner.

World grammar: satin bottle-green structures, cool limestone surfaces, brushed aluminium, translucent glass, one restrained vermilion-pink signal accent, soft warm-white studio light, crisp reflections, no people, no text, no logos, no UI.

Film camera: one unbroken slow push-in with a gentle lateral orbit, stable exposure, minimal motion blur, center-safe composition and clean negative space.

Mobile framing: keep the main subject within the middle 55% of frame; mobile uses a lighter crop of the same film.

Delivery budget: desktop <=32 MiB; mobile <=16 MiB.

Locked palette:
- Signal Green #163A34
- Limestone #E8E9E2
- Vermilion Pink #F03D5E
- Ink #17211F
- Soft White #F7F8F4

Palette defense: deep green carries institutional authority while the restrained vermilion-pink signal adds creative energy without common AI neon-blue, orange-on-black, beige-luxury or purple-glow defaults.

Locked type: Arial Narrow / Helvetica Neue Condensed for display statements; Helvetica Neue / Arial for body and data.

Section plan:
1. Scroll journey - cinematic full-viewport company introduction.
2. Authority strip - legal-status and company data rhythm.
3. Company premise - asymmetric editorial statement.
4. Capability architecture - five connected communication disciplines.
5. Signal plate - full-bleed visual identity break.
6. Company dossier - structured legal facts, addresses and operating details.
7. Registered activities - full business-code disclosure.
8. Contact - direct email, phone and business address.
9. Footer - legal entity identity.

Asset plan:
- One generated architectural hero/cover visual.
- One generated storyboard reference.
- One scroll-controlled film derived from the approved visual if API video generation is unavailable.
- Desktop + mobile MP4s with exact first-frame posters.
- Wordmark and simple H/16 monogram.
- Favicon/head icon kit.
- OG image and marketplace cover.
- No fictional portfolio, clients, testimonials, awards or performance metrics.

CTA inventory:
- Registry verification link with drawing underline.
- Email CTA as a wide architectural frame with flood-fill interaction.
- Call CTA as a vermilion data block with number-tracking response.
No shared site-wide button treatment.

Copy rules:
- English only.
- Legal names and supplied official data remain exact where useful for identification.
- Use both supplied addresses with explicit labels.
- Present Advertising as the registered primary business line.
- Credibility comes from company data and operating scope, not invented proof.

Anti-convergence ledger: first website build in this conversation; no previous website identity axes to avoid repeating.
